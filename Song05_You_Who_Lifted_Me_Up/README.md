# You Who Lifted Me Up - என்னை கைதூக்கி எடுத்தவரே

This song of gratitude and unshakable joy echoes Psalm 30 — weeping may tarry for the night, but joy comes with the morning. The Lord turns mourning into dancing, lifts from the pit, and fills with gladness. In the eternal day, this joy is perfected — no more night, only endless morning with the Lamb (Revelation 21:4; 22:5).
Closing Prayer for the Listener
Lord Jesus Christ, who lifts us from the pit and turns weeping to joy,
thank You for Your healing touch and unshakable peace.
Let our hearts rejoice in Your presence always.
In the eternal day, let us dance before You forever.
Come quickly, our Joy and Redeemer.
