# I Commit My Spirit - என் ஆவியை ஒப்புவிக்கிறேன்

This song of total surrender echoes Psalm 31 — “Into your hand I commit my spirit; you have redeemed me, O Lord, faithful God.” Jesus quoted these words on the cross (Luke 23:46), fulfilling them perfectly. It is intimate trust in trial — committing spirit and breath to God’s hand. In the eternal day, this surrender is complete — we rest forever in His faithful hands.
Closing Prayer for the Listener
Lord Jesus Christ, who committed Your spirit into the Father’s hand,
thank You for redeeming us and giving us strength.
Let us commit our spirit and breath to You in every trial.
In the eternal day, hold us forever in Your hand.
Come quickly, our faithful God.
