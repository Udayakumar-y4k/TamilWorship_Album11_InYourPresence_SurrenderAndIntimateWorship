# I Wait for the Lord - கர்த்தருக்கே காத்திருப்பேன்

This song of patient waiting and confident trust echoes Psalm 27 — “The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?” It is a cry for God’s face, protection in His tabernacle, and victory over enemies. In the eternal day, this waiting is fulfilled — we dwell in His presence, seeing His face forever (Revelation 22:4).
Closing Prayer for the Listener
Lord Jesus Christ, our Light and Salvation,
thank You for being our fortress when all else fails.
Let us wait for You with joyful hearts.
Hide us in Your tabernacle, lift our heads.
Come quickly — let us dwell in Your presence forever.
